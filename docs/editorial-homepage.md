# Editorial homepage

The About page now acts as a landing page for the rest of the site. A large
greeting and portrait establish the introduction, followed by a readable bio and
three direct links into development, photography, and sports. A contact link
provides a clear next step for collaboration.

The card grid collapses to a single column on smaller screens. New labels and
descriptions are translated in English and Russian, the two locales enabled by
`src/i18n/routing.ts`. The existing visual identity remains in place, with a
restrained yellow accent on the greeting and section icons.

See the [feature specification](./specs/editorial-homepage.md) for the goals and
acceptance criteria.
